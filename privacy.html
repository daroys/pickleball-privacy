Pickleball League Manager — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Pickleball League Manager ("the app") helps organizers run pickleball leagues: keeping a player roster, creating matches, recording scores and ranking players. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

The app does not collect your personal information. Everything you enter stays on your own device. We have no accounts, no servers that receive your data, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

What the app stores on your device

To work, the app saves the information you type in: league names and settings, player names, genders, rankings, scores and game records. This data is stored locally on your device only. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us or to any third party.

What we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in or registration is required.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics services, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Exporting your data

When you choose to save or share a league's results (for example "Save League Data"), the app creates a CSV file and hands it to your device's share or save sheet. Where that file goes (Files, AirDrop, Mail and so on) is your choice, and the destination you pick handles it under its own policies. The app does not send it anywhere on its own.

Your data and deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you control it. Deleting a league in the app removes that league's data. Deleting the app removes all of the app's data from your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, for example if optional online features are added, this policy will be updated and the date above will change.
